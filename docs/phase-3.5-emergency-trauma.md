# Phase 3.5 — Emergency & Trauma

## Implemented scope

Emergency visits are hospital- and department-scoped. The module supports arrival registration, manual triage and reassessment, assigned clinician treatment, append-only care events and signed notes, and disposition.

### Workflow

1. Register emergency arrival with patient, destination department, arrival mode and chief complaint.
2. A designated emergency nurse records the initial manual triage level, red-flag note and available observations.
3. Reassessment appends a new triage revision; prior triage entries are retained.
4. Emergency clinicians take waiting cases and receive an encounter-scoped patient-care assignment.
5. Assigned nurses and clinicians append emergency care events and signed notes.
6. The assigned clinician records discharge, admission request, referral or transfer request. An emergency nurse may record a patient leaving without being seen before treatment begins.
7. The encounter and assignments are updated; downstream inpatient admission may be requested through the Inpatient module.

## API routes

| Method | Path | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| POST | `/api/v1/emergency/visits` | Register emergency arrival |
| GET | `/api/v1/emergency/visits` | View role-scoped emergency queue |
| GET | `/api/v1/emergency/visits/{visit_id}` | Read visit and clinical event history |
| POST | `/api/v1/emergency/visits/{visit_id}/triage` | Record initial triage or reassessment |
| POST | `/api/v1/emergency/visits/{visit_id}/take` | Clinician takes a waiting case |
| POST | `/api/v1/emergency/visits/{visit_id}/events` | Append assessment/intervention/procedure/reassessment/handover event |
| POST | `/api/v1/emergency/visits/{visit_id}/notes` | Sign an emergency note |
| POST | `/api/v1/emergency/visits/{visit_id}/disposition` | Close the emergency visit with disposition |

## Authorization and audit

- Registration roles can register arrivals and view the hospital emergency queue.
- Emergency nurses can view open cases in their department and record triage/reassessment.
- Clinicians can view cases only with an active encounter assignment; they must take a waiting case before documenting treatment.
- Only assigned clinicians can record discharge, admission request, referral or transfer request.
- Emergency nurses can record left-without-being-seen only while the visit is arrived/waiting.
- Hospital RLS and composite foreign keys bind visits to the same hospital, patient, encounter and department.
- Triage history, care events and notes are append-only through the API.
- Audit metadata records actor/action/resource only, not clinical notes, triage narrative or vital values.

## Clinical safety boundaries

- Triage acuity is entered by qualified staff. No automatic scoring, AI urgency assignment or diagnosis is applied.
- Red-flag presence requires an escalation note and cannot be paired with less-urgent/non-urgent triage.
- Vital ranges are broad data-quality checks, not diagnostic reference ranges.
- Disposition `admission_requested` is not a confirmed admission or bed; use the separate Inpatient acceptance and bed-allocation workflow.
- `referred` and `transfer_requested` are local dispositions only; they do not confirm external acceptance, record sharing or ambulance dispatch.
- No resuscitation protocol, medication administration, procedure order, imaging/lab order, mortality certification or autonomous action is generated by this module.
- Emergency AI capabilities remain governed by central AI release gates and are not activated here.

## Deferred integrations

- Ambulance dispatch, vehicle tracking, paramedic handover and inter-hospital transfer coordination.
- Specialty trauma protocols, resuscitation checklists and device/monitor integrations.
- Emergency medication orders and medication administration through the Pharmacy module.
- Laboratory, radiology, surgery, ICU, maternity and pediatrics workflows.
- Clinical addenda/corrections, staffing rosters and bed capacity dashboards.

All state transitions, triage revision, red-flag, assignment, tenant, access, audit and disposition checks are listed in the Phase 5 Test Register. Tests have not been executed for this milestone.
